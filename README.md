# 📈 Sales Forecasting & Demand Prediction using Power BI

An end-to-end Business Intelligence project that analyzes historical retail sales data to uncover business insights, identify demand patterns, and forecast future sales using Power BI's built-in forecasting capabilities.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project demonstrates the complete analytics workflow—from SQL data cleaning and preparation to building an interactive Power BI dashboard for sales analysis and demand forecasting.

The dashboard enables users to monitor sales performance, analyze trends, compare historical performance, and predict future sales to support data-driven business decisions.


## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 📌 Dashboard 1 – Business Overview

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Total Quantity
- Sales by Market
- Sales by Category
- Sales by Segment
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Top 10 Products by Sales

---

### 📌 Dashboard 2 – Forecasting & Trend Analysis

- Monthly Sales Trend with 12-Month Forecast
- Previous Year Sales
- YoY Growth %
- Annual Sales Performance
- Forecast Period
- Interactive Filters (Year, Category, Market, Segment)
- Key Business Insights Panel

## 📈 Key Insights

- Total Sales reached **12.64M**.
- APAC is the highest-performing market.
- Technology is the best-performing product category.
- Consumer is the highest-performing customer segment.
- Sales show a consistent upward trend from 2011 to 2014.
- December recorded the highest sales among all months.
- Forecast analysis indicates continued sales growth.
